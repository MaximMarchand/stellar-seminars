For the Speakers

Here are some informations for the people presenting to the Stellar Seminars.

  • The Stellar Seminars take place every Thursday between 10h00 and 11h00. Usually, 45 minutes are dedicated to the talk, and the remaining 15 minutes are dedicated to questions from the audience.
  • The audience consists of PHD/Master students, postdocs and professors working in different areas of stellar physics. Note that some of the people may not be totally familiar with your research topic.
  • We let the possibility for people who cannot attend the seminar in person to join us via a Zoom session. Usually, the speaker connects to the session and shares his/her screen in order to make his/her slides visible for the people attending remotely.
  • If you agree, your talk will be recorded and made available via a private link that is given only to the Stellar Group members.
  • We usually send an email to announce the seminar on Monday. Please send a title and abstract to Maxime Marchand at least one week before your talk.
